8. You need a Social Security Number (SSN) if you are an international student who has a job or a job offer. An SSN is required for reporting your wages to the government and determining eligibility for Social Security benefits. 

- If you do not have a job or job offer, you cannot apply for an SSN.
- If you have received scholarships or grants but are not employed, you may need a different tax-related identification number instead of an SSN.

For those who need to apply for an SSN, you will need:

- I-20 (printed and signed)
- I-94 (printed copy of your Arrival/Departure Record)
- Passport
- Employment letter signed by your job supervisor and an ISE staff member
- Application Reference Number from your Online Social Security Number Application

Please note that the SSN is considered sensitive information. Once obtained, keep the card in a safe place and do not share it via email. If you already have an Individual Tax Identification Number (ITIN) and receive an SSN, you should notify the IRS to merge your accounts.
